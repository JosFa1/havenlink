Meet Our Founders

Asad Sadikov

Co-Founder

Asad Sadikov is a Co-Founder and lead technologist at HavenLink with strong expertise in data analysis, machine learning, and scalable system design. He builds the analytics and matching infrastructure that powers fair and privacy-preserving adoption workflows, turning complex datasets into actionable insights. Asad is passionately committed to children’s equality and uses technical rigor to ensure the platform improves safety, access, and equitable outcomes for families and children.

Aarav Gupta

Co-Founder

Aarav Gupta is the Co-Founder of HavenLink, a platform focused on making adoption more accessible, transparent, and family-centered through technology. Driven by a passion for civic impact and innovation, he helps lead HavenLink's mission to connect adoptive families with trusted agencies and resources while simplifying the adoption journey. Outside of HavenLink, Aarav enjoys trying new restaurants, spending time with friends, and competing in debate, where he has earned recognition and rankings at both the state and national level.
