Home Study Service

A home study is a readiness assessment performed by a licensed social worker. It helps agencies confirm that your home provides a safe, stable, and nurturing environment for a child.

Start Family Intake

What happens during a home study?

A licensed social worker will review household composition, background checks, finances, references, and your readiness to parent. The process helps identify supports you may need and prepares your family for a successful placement.

Why it matters

Home studies are required by most agencies and by interstate rules. Completing a home study early can speed matching, help with agency routing, and clarify required steps for legal readiness.

Next step

When you’re ready, proceed to the Family Intake Form where we collect more detail to help match you with appropriate agencies and children.
