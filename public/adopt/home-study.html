Home Study Service

Step 1 of Your Adoption Journey

What is a Home Study?

A home study is a comprehensive evaluation conducted by a licensed social worker to assess your family's readiness for adoption. It's one of the most important steps in the adoption process and is required by law in every state.

The home study process helps ensure that your home provides a safe, stable, and supportive environment for an adopted child, and gives you confidence that you're prepared for the unique joys and challenges of adoption.

What to Expect

1

Application & Interview

You'll complete an application and have an initial conversation with your social worker to discuss your family's background, values, and goals.

2

In-Home Visit

A social worker will visit your home to assess its safety, stability, and suitability for a child. They'll review bedrooms, childproofing, and space for an adopted child.

3

Background Checks

Criminal background checks and child welfare checks will be conducted for all adults in the household to ensure child safety.

4

Adoption Training

You may be required to complete adoption education (such as PRIDE training) to prepare you for the emotional and developmental needs of adopted children.

5

Report & Approval

Your social worker will prepare a comprehensive home study report for submission to adoption agencies and courts.

Why It Matters

Child Safety

Protects vulnerable children by ensuring they'll be placed in safe, stable homes.

Legal Requirement

Required by law in all 50 states and necessary to proceed with adoption.

Family Preparation

Helps you understand your readiness and prepares you for the adoption journey ahead.

Quick Facts

  • Duration: 2-6 months typically
  • Cost: $500-$3,000 USD (varies by state)
  • Required For: All adoptions (domestic and international)
  • Who Conducts: Licensed social workers or agencies
  • Validity: Usually valid for 1-2 years
