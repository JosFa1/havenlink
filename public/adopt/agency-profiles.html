Agency‑Managed Profiles

Licensed agencies create and manage child profiles on HavenLink. These profiles include structured, non-identifying information that helps families and agencies determine fit.

Continue to Intake

How agencies publish profiles

Agency-managed profiles are reviewed and redacted to remove identifying details. They provide age ranges, medical & developmental needs, family contact preferences, and non-identifying history.

Privacy & verification

Only verified, licensed agencies can publish profiles. Families contact agencies through secure, agency-mediated workflows to protect children's privacy.

Next steps

Complete the Family Intake to be matched with relevant agency profiles and to see the recommended agencies for your case.
