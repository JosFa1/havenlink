Agency-Managed Profiles

Step 3 of Your Adoption Journey

Licensed Agencies, Verified Profiles

Every licensed adoption agency on HavenLink is thoroughly verified and meets strict regulatory standards. This ensures that child profiles posted on our platform come from legitimate, accountable organizations dedicated to child welfare.

How Agency Profiles Work

1

Verified Agency Partners

Agencies undergo detailed vetting to confirm their licensure, safety record, and commitment to ethical practices in child placement.

2

Comprehensive Child Profiles

Agencies create detailed, accurate profiles that include a child's background, needs, personality, interests, and readiness for placement.

3

Profile Management Tools

Agencies use HavenLink's tools to update profiles, manage inquiries, and track agency-family communications securely.

4

Privacy & Safety First

Child profile information is protected. Direct contact between families and children does not occur on HavenLink—the agency manages all communication.

5

Ongoing Compliance

Agencies are regularly audited to ensure compliance with state laws and best practices in child welfare.

What a Well-Curated Profile Includes

Personal Background

Age, birth siblings, ethnic and cultural background, family history, and prior placements if any.

Health & Development

Medical history, developmental milestones, special needs, and current health status.

Personality & Interests

Temperament, hobbies, strengths, challenges, and social preferences.

Support Needs

Recommendations for family structure, therapeutic support, school settings, and ongoing resources.
