Compatibility Matching

Our matching system is a deterministic, explainable scoring algorithm that weighs your intake answers to recommend child matches and agencies.

Review Intake

Deterministic scoring

The score is computed from explicit inputs: age preference, special needs openness, sibling openness, trauma readiness, gender preference, experience, and other factors. No AI is used. Each factor shows a clear contribution to the final score.

Sample score card

82
Match Strength:

Strong — good alignment on age and support services; consider agencies experienced with therapeutic placements.

What influences the score

  • Age range alignment
  • Openess to medical/developmental needs
  • Sibling openness
  • Trauma readiness
  • Experience with fostering/adoption
