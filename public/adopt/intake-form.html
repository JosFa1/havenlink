Interstate adoption (ICPC) applies Your residence and the adoption state differ, so this will be an interstate placement governed by the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children (ICPC). It adds a review step between the two states' agencies — your assigned agency will guide you through it.

What is the ICPC?

The ICPC is an agreement among all 50 states, DC, and the U.S. Virgin Islands that protects children placed across state lines. Both the sending and receiving state must approve the placement before the child can move, which typically adds a few weeks to the timeline.

What is a Home Study?

A home study is a written assessment by a licensed social worker confirming your home is a safe, stable place for a child. It usually includes interviews, background checks, a home visit, and reference checks.

  • Estimated cost: typically $1,000–$3,000 (often reduced or waived in foster-to-adopt cases).
  • Typical timeline: about 2–6 months from start to approval.
