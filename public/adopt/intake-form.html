Registry note — interstate adoption

Your residence and adoption state differ, so the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children (ICPC) applies. Both states must approve the placement before a child can move — routine, but it typically adds a few weeks. Your agency will guide the review.

Learn more about the ICPC

Pre-filled from your residence. Families sometimes adopt through another state — that is routine.

Registry note — what is a home study?

A written assessment by a licensed social worker confirming your home is a safe, stable place for a child — interviews, background checks, a home visit, and references. Typically $1,000–$3,000 and 2–6 months, often reduced in foster-to-adopt cases.

You do not need one to continue this form. Visit our Home Study service
