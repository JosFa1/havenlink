Step 04 — Post-Adoption Resource Finder

Find support near you

Adoption is a lifelong journey. Enter your ZIP code and choose the kinds of help you're looking for — counseling, trauma support, financial assistance, support groups, medical care, or educational services — to see resources in your area on the map below.

Listings are drawn from public directories (SAMHSA behavioral-health facilities, HRSA health centers) and a small curated set. Locations for some listings are approximate (mapped to the center of their ZIP code). Always confirm services, hours, and eligibility directly with the provider before relying on a listing.
