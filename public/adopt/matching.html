Compatibility Matching

Step 4 of Your Adoption Journey

Finding Your Perfect Match

The HavenLink matching system uses deterministic, data-driven scoring to suggest matches between your family profile and available children. Our algorithm considers multiple factors about your family, your preferences, and each child's needs—not guesswork or artificial intelligence, but transparent logic designed to support better outcomes.

How Our Matching Works

Our matching process is built on transparency. Every match suggestion includes a detailed breakdown of the compatibility score and the factors that influenced it. We never hide the reasoning—you'll understand exactly why we suggest a particular pairing.

1

Your Family Profile

From your intake form, we understand your household, values, experience, and preferences for a child's age, needs, and background.

2

Child Profile Data

Licensed agencies provide rich, detailed information about each child: age, developmental stage, health, personality, interests, and support needs.

3

Algorithmic Scoring

Our deterministic algorithm calculates a compatibility score (0-100) by analyzing alignment on age preference, special needs openness, sibling placement, trauma readiness, and other key factors.

4

Transparent Results

We show you the match score, the scoring breakdown, and the agency responsible for the child's profile. You can then reach out to the agency to explore the match further.

5

Agency Communication

Once you've expressed interest, HavenLink facilitates communication between you and the agency. The agency manages all next steps, including visits and legal processes.

Important Notes on Matching

  • Not AI-Driven: Our scoring is deterministic and based on explicit logic, not machine learning. We believe transparency in matching is essential.
  • Not a Guarantee: A high match score suggests good compatibility, but final placement decisions involve many factors, including agency assessment and legal considerations.
  • You Choose: You see all match suggestions and decide which to pursue. There's no automatic assignment.
  • Agency Led: Once you express interest, the licensed agency takes the lead in assessing fit and guiding the placement process.
  • Ongoing Support: Successful adoption continues after placement. We recommend post-adoption support resources for all families.
