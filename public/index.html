    Whereas,
    HavenLink is a free civic technology platform founded in 2025 by students at Trinity Preparatory School in Winter Park to provide prospective adoptive parents with a guided path from their first questions through the day a child comes home, while connecting families directly with licensed adoption agencies; and
    Whereas,
    HavenLink has remained completely free for every family it serves and has assisted more than 1,000 users while connecting over 200 families with licensed adoption agencies throughout 32 states and five countries; and
    Whereas,
    HavenLink collaborates with more than 30 licensed adoption agencies and has established partnerships with UNICEF and the United Nations Population Fund, demonstrating the far-reaching impact of a locally founded initiative; and
    Whereas,
    HavenLink is led by Technical Director Asad Sadikov and Nontechnical Director Aarav Gupta, whose vision, leadership, and commitment have transformed a high school project into a nationally recognized platform that continues to serve families seeking to build loving and permanent homes through adoption; and
    Whereas,
    HavenLink has strengthened awareness of adoption by engaging with local governments, state legislative offices, community organizations, and the media, while promoting access to reliable adoption resources and advocating for families across Florida and beyond; and
