Connecting Children to Loving Families Through Technology

HavenLink is a secure, structured platform that bridges prospective adoptive families with licensed adoption agencies. We make the adoption journey clearer, faster, and more human.

Our Mission

HavenLink was built to transform the adoption process into a secure, transparent, and family-centered experience. We believe that connecting children with loving families should be faster, clearer, and more human.

Secure & Structured Matching

A transparent, data-driven process that ensures compatibility between families and available children.

Licensed Agency Verification

Every partner agency on HavenLink is thoroughly vetted and licensed, ensuring compliance and accountability.

Family-Centered Design

Built with sensitivity to the emotional journey of adoptive families, with empathy at every step.

The Challenge

The adoption process today is often fragmented across multiple platforms, agencies, and paperwork systems. Families struggle to navigate the complexity, verify which agencies are legitimate, and communicate clearly throughout their journey.

HavenLink consolidates this experience into one secure, well-structured platform—letting families focus on what matters: building their family.

How It Works

Four simple steps to connect families with their perfect match

Home Study Service

A comprehensive assessment by a licensed social worker evaluates your home, background, finances, and readiness to adopt, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for a child.

03

Agency-Managed Profiles

Built with Child Safety & Privacy First

HavenLink is designed with the highest standards of security and legal compliance

No Direct Family-to-Child Contact

All communication flows through verified agencies to protect child safety and privacy.

Verified Agency Partners

Every partner agency is thoroughly vetted and licensed by child welfare authorities.

Secure Authentication

Multi-factor authentication and industry-standard encryption protect all user accounts and data.

Privacy-Focused Data Handling

Data minimization, anonymization, and strict compliance with FERPA, COPPA, and adoption privacy laws.

Moderated Community Spaces

Any discussion areas are actively moderated to maintain safety, respect, and appropriateness.
